    Road to the Platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough — no mission replay, free-roam after the story. The platinum pops the moment the other 54 are yours.

    Before you start

    • One possibly-missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate with Lucia at Jason's house in Chapter 1, before that chapter ends — it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy needs that one, so don't skip it.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Leave them off for the whole run.
    • Keep chapter saves. There is no mission replay, so hold a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.
    • Nothing else is missable. Every quest giver is still around in free-roam after the story, and optional missions that expire never count toward a trophy.
