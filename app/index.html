    How this guide is built

    • Names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them; every guide is written in our own words and names its sources beside it.
    • Sources: Exophase and PSNProfiles for the official list; KeenGamer, Insider Gaming, GameMapa and EGamers.io for requirements, the 100% checklist and the missable warning. Five hidden trophies are still unconfirmed by the community — their guides say so plainly instead of guessing.
    • Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device. Export writes a JSON file; import reads one back.
    • Nothing here is Rockstar Games artwork, logo or box art. The look is our own.
