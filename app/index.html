⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 Meditation Spots. After an early Chapter 1 mission, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house one morning and heads out to the balcony. Follow her and accept the prompt. The balcony is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the others, and guide writers have not confirmed whether the trophy needs it — but you cannot do it again after Chapter 1, so do it. Nothing else in the list can be missed.

Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours in a single playthrough. There is no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of every chapter — the game gives you 15 manual slots. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 hours

    Play the story and pick up optional missions as they appear. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the morning Lucia offers it. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars as you go: 100% needs 200 of the 277 vehicle models and 100 of the 255 animals. Everything you do before the ending already counts toward the checklist.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & Freaks about 10 hours

    Finish the stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — eight strands in all for the silver. The 100% checklist does not ask for these quests, so they are usually what is left after the story, together with any stray trophies that did not pop on their own.

  3. Stage 3 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the ending, Progress, then Stats, gains a 100% Completion entry. You need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so skip the nastiest ones. Most side quests are not required, and collectibles only partly.

  4. Stage 4 — Cleanup and the platinum a few hours

    Mop up what is left: 250 of the 397 stunts, 3 of the 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 50 of the 80 Challenges. When the checklist hits 100%, A Long Road pops, and with it the platinum, Vice Got a Price!
